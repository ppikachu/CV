Luz Negra is a multidisciplinary studio specializing in animation, motion graphics, and interactive design. The goal was to create a portfolio experience that immediately signals their creative capabilities through an interactive, stylized 3D environment.

![Luz Negra](/images/web_luznegra-1620x769.png)

## 3D Environment & Assets
Modeled custom stylized 3D assets in GLTF format, including a retro drive-in cinema setup with interactive elements.

## Three.js Scene Engineering
Built a reactive WebGL scene featuring day/night lighting toggle and camera movement, running at smooth framerates across devices.

## Front-End & Content Management
Developed the frontend in Nuxt with a headless Contentful CMS pipeline for dynamic project updates.

## Performance Optimization
Optimized geometry, materials, and textures for seamless mobile and desktop performance.
