Design, development, and deployment of the regional digital hub for Great Lengths Latin America — the premium hair extensions brand.

![Great Lengths](/images/web_GL-1620x769.png)

## Interactive Geolocation
Integrated Google Maps API to provide an intuitive salon locator that maps certified salons across the continent — the core user-facing feature of the platform.

## Architecture & Deployment
Built with Nuxt and Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel for fast global delivery and strong mobile performance.
