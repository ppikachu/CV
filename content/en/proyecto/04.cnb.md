Redesigned the interactive kiosk interface used by museum visitors to browse the Bicentennial digital archive — a collection of historical photographs, videos, and news organized into national milestones.

![Archivo digital](/images/cnb4-1024x639.jpg)

## Kiosk UX/UI
Redesigned the visitor-facing interface for clarity and ease of use, working within the museum's existing design standards and physical kiosk hardware.

## Custom CMS Theme
Developed a custom theme for Omeka, an open-source digital collection management system, integrating it with the museum's approved visual identity.

## Archive Structuring
Organized and added a large volume of archival material — photographs, video, and press coverage — into the permanent collection, structured by historical milestones and searchable by visitors.
