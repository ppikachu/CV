## Head in the Clouds - Animated Series

::youtube{:url="https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLeb5KurR3ZBDR76X59DFt3Ow2n2aDoGu6"}
::

Short-form animated series co-produced by Luz Negra and Pequeño Editor for the Pakapaka Channel. Finalist of the PRIX JEUNESSE 2020.

Photos of a model were used for the protagonist's head, combined with a 3D-modeled body to generate rig views. Different costumes were created for each episode.

![Eli](/images/eli-2757x1020.jpg)

- 3D modeling and rendering of character components
- Character rigging in Moho
- Editing, color correction, and broadcast mastering
- Collaboration with directors on animation direction
- Technical support during the animation pipeline
