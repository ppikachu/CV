## The Challenge
Sanders required an elegant, high-impact digital presence that would convey sophistication, modern styling, and the premium quality of their salon services and proprietary hair care line.

## My Role & Execution
* **Art Direction & Visual Language:** Defined a sleek, editorial aesthetic that balances luxury typography with generous negative space.
* **Creative Coding & GLSL:** Engineered custom GLSL shader effects and fluid transitions to elevate the interactive feel without compromising performance.
* **Front-End Architecture:** Built with Nuxt and deployed on Vercel for lightning-fast worldwide delivery and high SEO visibility.
* **Content Strategy:** Structured service menus, product showcases, and clear booking pathways.
