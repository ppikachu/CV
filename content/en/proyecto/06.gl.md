## Overview
Planning, design, development, and infrastructure deployment for Great Lengths Latin America's official regional hub.

* **Interactive Geolocation:** Integration with Google Maps API to provide an intuitive salon locator showing certified salons across the continent.
* **Architecture & Deployment:** Built with Nuxt and Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel with high uptime and mobile performance.
