## Overview
Visual identity in motion for Susan Ibitz, an international behavioral analyst, profiler, and speaker. Over an ongoing 9+ year collaboration, I oversee the end-to-end audiovisual direction and visual content for her global digital channels.

## My Role & Contribution
* **Motion Graphics & Title Sequences:** Designed dynamic motion assets, lower-thirds, animated infographics, and episodic bumpers.
* **Video Editing & Broadcast Post-Production:** Full video editing, color grading, and audio mastering for YouTube, keynote presentations, and international training programs.
* **Visual Synthesis & AI Exploration:** Created custom visual assets and conceptual illustrations using Stable Diffusion and digital drawing to explain complex psychological and non-verbal communication concepts.
