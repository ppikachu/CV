## Con la cabeza en las nubes - Head in the clouds (Animated series)

![Eli](/images/Eli_psd_personaje_v02-1920x1080.jpg)

Short lenght series coproduced by Luz Negra and Pequeño Editor for the Pakapaka Channel. Finalist of the PRIX JEUNESSE 2020.

Photos of a model for the protagonist's head and a 3D modeled body were used to generate rig views. Different clothes were used in each episode.

![Eli](/images/eli-2757x1020.jpg)

- 3D modeling and rendering of the parts that make up the main protagonist
- Character rigged in Moho
- Editing, color correction and apto mastering Broadcast
- Collaborated with the directors and the rest of the creatives in outlining and achieving the animation goals
- Provided technical support during animation process
