## The Concept
Luz Negra is a multidisciplinary studio specializing in animation, motion graphics, and interactive design. The goal was to create a portfolio experience that immediately signals their creative capabilities through an interactive, stylized 3D environment.

## My Role & Technical Execution
* **3D Environment & Assets:** Modeled custom low-poly/stylized 3D assets in GLTF format, including a retro drive-in cinema setup.
* **Three.js Scene Engineering:** Built a reactive WebGL scene featuring day/night lighting toggles, camera movements, and interactive hotspots.
* **Front-End & Content Management:** Developed the frontend in Nuxt with a headless Contentful CMS pipeline for dynamic project updates.
* **Performance Optimization:** Optimized geometry, materials, and textures for seamless mobile and desktop framerates.
