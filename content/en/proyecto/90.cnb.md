Using the existing database, redesigned the museum kiosk interface (UI, UX) accessible by visitors.

Developed a custom theme matching the museum design standard using 'Omeka' (open-source digital collection management system).

A large amount of photographs, videos and news (organized into historical milestones and open for search) was added to the permanent collection.
