## The Experiment
An interactive perpetual motion illusion exploring gravity, mass differentials, and material shaders in the browser.

Two geometric bodies float in zero-friction space. One body has significantly more mass than the other, generating a mesmerizing rhythm of momentum transfer, anticipation, and kinetic energy.

* **Real-time WebGL Rendering:** Built with Three.js and custom GLSL lighting shaders.
* **Physics & Momentum Math:** Precise momentum exchange calculations without external heavy physics engines.
* **Interactive Camera & Controls:** Full 3D orbital navigation and lighting parameters.
